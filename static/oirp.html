    Where the numbers come from

    Two kinds of number, never mixed

    Every answer is labelled with where its numbers came from. The two are never compared with each other: one is what Carleton published, the other is invented data shaped like the institutional warehouse.

    ✓Published source

    What Carleton published

    Real figures, checked by hand against public reports (NSSE 2023, annual and budget reports), each with its file and page.

    How it answers

    Rules it keeps, every time

    The assistant reads the same definitions an analyst would, and it asks the data through governed measures rather than writing its own SQL.

    No invented numbers

    Every number comes from a lookup. If the published figure isn't there, it says so and names the report that has it.

    Says what was counted

    Which count date, which term, which cohort year, and whether a student is placed in the faculty they entered or the one they're in now.

    Small groups stay hidden

    A cell with fewer than ten students is suppressed, and suppressed is never read as zero.

    Says how fresh it is

    Each cube reports when it was last loaded. When one is behind, the answer gives its date.
